Text for the Steam page (and itch.io). Written for players, in English. No dashes, no big words.

## Short description (max 300 characters)

Live one life, year by year, from the first breath to the last. Then carry on as your child. A
family story across generations, where the choices you make and the people you hurt are
remembered long after you are gone.

## About this game

**One More Year** is a life told one year at a time. You are born in Sweden or the United States
in the 1950s, 70s or 90s. You go to school, fall in love, find work, lose it, have children, make
money or lose it all. Every year brings a few moments that ask something of you. There is no right
answer, only a life.

When you die, the story goes on. You choose who carries it: a daughter, a nephew, a grandchild.
They inherit your money, your house, your debts and your reputation. They also remember how you
treated them.

**A family that remembers.** People keep what you do to them. The brother you never visited, the
mother who drank, the father who paid for university. Memories turn into grudges and loyalties
that shape the next generation, and the one after that.

**Who you are matters.** Traits change your chances and what you notice. A charming person gets out
of trouble in other ways than a hot tempered one. A paranoid one sees what others miss, and things
that are not there.

**History happens around you.** Real events of each decade, from the record years to the phone in
every pocket, and a future that keeps going for as long as your family does. The look and the
music change with the times.

**Money that means something.** Salaries, tax, rent and mortgages that follow each country and
decade. Buy a home, a summer house, a family seat. Give it away in the family name. Or gamble it.

**Dark when it has to be.** Addiction, violence, crime and prison are part of some lives. You decide
how much of it you want to see.

## Features

- Two countries, Sweden and the USA, from 1950 into the future, with emigration between them
- Nearly 1,200 events, from first words to last wishes
- 32 career paths, including creative and political careers where fame comes and goes
- Families that grow, split and remember across generations
- Generated portraits that inherit features and age with the years
- A family newspaper, a photo album for every life, a family tree and a chronicle
- Dreams for a whole life, wishes for each year, and close to 40 achievements
- Content settings for every dark theme
